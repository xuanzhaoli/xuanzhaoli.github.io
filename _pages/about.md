I am currently a Ph.D. student at the Institute of Genetics and Developmental Biology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, supervised by Prof.[Fei He](https://people.ucas.ac.cn/~feihe), since Fall 2023.
My research focuses on wheat genomics and the patterns of gene expression changes during distant hybridization.
Prior to starting my Ph.D., I participated in several large-scale genomics projects as both a lead contributor and team member, including the rice pan-genome project and the locust genome project. I expect to graduate in 2027 and am currently seeking suitable career opportunities.
